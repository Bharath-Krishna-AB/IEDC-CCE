## 1. IMPACT 2026 — Faculty Development Program

- **Type:** Faculty Development Program (5 days)
- **Date:** 22–23 June 2026 (offline, CCE) + 24–26 June 2026 (online)
- **Venue:** Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), Irinjalakuda
- **In association with:** IIITM-K, Wadhwani Foundation, Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM), Thrissur Management Association (TMA)
- **Audience:** IEDC Nodal Officers and faculty interested in entrepreneurship education (33 faculty from colleges across Kerala registered)
- **Coordinators:** Aswathy P Sajeev (Nodal Officer); Merin Joy, Antony K C (Student)

**Teaser:** A five-day FDP empowering educators with entrepreneurial thinking, innovation methodologies and strategies to build innovation ecosystems in their institutions.

**Overview:** IMPACT 2026 brought together academic leaders, entrepreneurs, industry experts and ecosystem enablers for two days of in-person sessions followed by three days online. The inauguration featured Er. Christo George (Chairman & MD, Hykon India) as Chief Guest and Mr. Seejo Ponnore Jose (MD & Founder, Aquastar Plastic) as Guest of Honour. Sessions led by Wadhwani Foundation, IIITM-K, KSUM and Bhashini covered problem identification, customer discovery, MVP validation, business modelling, go-to-market strategy, AI-enabled entrepreneurship tools and pitching. The online days focused on marketing, finance, team building and venture scaling, concluding with venture-viability and pedagogy-demonstration presentations by participant teams.

**Speakers:** Er. Christo George (Hykon India), Mr. Seejo Ponnore Jose (Aquastar Plastic), Mr. Gopakumar Vishwanathan (Wadhwani Foundation), Mr. Padmakumar C (President, TMA), Mr. Nasif N M (KSUM), Mr. Sebin Sunny (IIITM-K), Mr. Ajith P Suresh (Bhashini)

## 5. Git 'n' GitHub — Beginner Workshop

- **Type:** Technical workshop (1 hour)
- **Date:** 11 August 2026, 2:30–3:30 PM
- **Venue:** IEDC Room, St. Chavara Block, CCE
- **Coordinators:** Dibin Shaju, Bharath Krishna A B, Antony K C
- **Audience:** Students with little or no prior experience with version control (~30 attended)

**Teaser:** From zero to your first push — a hands-on beginner workshop on Git and GitHub.

**Overview:** The workshop walked students through why version control matters and the core Git workflow — `init`, `add`, `commit`, `status`, `log` — with live terminal demos that participants followed on their own machines. The second half covered GitHub as a collaboration platform: creating repositories, connecting a local project, and using `push`, `pull` and `clone`. It closed with a Q&A on real-world use in academic and team projects.
